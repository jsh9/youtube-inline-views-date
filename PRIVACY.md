# Privacy Policy: YouTube Inline Views & Date

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

YouTube Inline Views & Date does not collect, store, or share any user data.

## What the extension does

On YouTube video pages (`https://www.youtube.com/watch...`), the extension reads the view count and publication date that YouTube has already loaded into the page. It then shows them next to the channel's Subscribe/Join buttons. All of this happens locally in your browser.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, record, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or page content.
- It does not store data in cookies, local storage, sync storage, or anywhere else.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- It does not sell or transfer any data to third parties.
- It requests no browser permissions, and it runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date.
